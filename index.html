ABOUT US

Chocolate Snackies was started in 2024 by 10 sous chefs working at Serena hotel. The positive reviews they got from their first customers led them into expanded operations. They specialised in Chocolate deserts as it stood out among local Bakeries so much so the business is now a household name. From supplying hotels, exclusive events and the general public, Chocolate snackies has displayed mastery in chocolate desserts.

Mission and Vision

Mission:

"To craft exquisite chocolate desserts that delight and satisfy our customers with quality, creativity, and passion."

Vision:

"To be the premier destination for chocolate lovers, renowned for rich, velvety treats and innovative flavors."

Know the Management.

Lugogobe Andrew is the CEO of Chocolate Snackies. He is a seasoned chocolateer with training from Culinary Arts Academy, Switzerland.
